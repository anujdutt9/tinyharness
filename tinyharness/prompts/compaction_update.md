The <prior-summary> covers everything before the <conversation>, and is discarded after this: anything you do
not carry into the new summary is lost. Write one summary that combines both: keep every goal, constraint,
finding and decision from the <prior-summary> that still holds, even if the conversation doesn't mention it;
where they conflict, the conversation is newer and wins. Reconcile execution state: move work to Completed
when the newer conversation shows it finished, and never leave that work in Active, Pending, or Next Action.
Completed Agent or Team calls remain completed. Choose one Next Action from the work that actually remains.
